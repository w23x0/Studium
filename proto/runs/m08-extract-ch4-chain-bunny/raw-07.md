| K35 | 变质量系统的动量平衡 | 原理 | 主体以恒定速度 $\mathbf v$ 运动、质量以速率 $dm/dt$ 汇入且入射速度为 $\mathbf u$ 时，所需外力为 $\mathbf F=(\mathbf v-\mathbf u)\,dm/dt$；以带符号的质量率表示时，流出对应负的 $dm/dt$。 | "Analyzing the forces on a system in which there is a flow of mass can be totally confusing if you try to apply Newton’s laws blindly." ‖ "can be either positive or negative, depending on the direction of the stream of mass." ‖ "The limiting procedure used in Example 4.12 expresses the physical situation correctly." | 资料抽取 |
| K36 | 同速质量流的零力条件 | 原理 | 若流入或流出的质量在穿越系统边界时与系统具有相同速度，则该质量流不改变系统总动量，在没有其他外力时维持匀速不需额外作用力。 | "Here the mass is decreasing. However, the velocity of the sand just after leaving the freight car is identical to its initial velocity, and its momentum does not change" | 资料抽取 |
| K37 | 短时段变质量系统法 | 方法 | 在有限短时段内选取覆盖流入或流出微元的系统，比较时段两端的总动量，除以时段长度并取极限，以得到瞬时动量流平衡。 | "The procedure of isolating the system, focusing on diferentials, and taking the limit may appear a trifle formal but it helps to avoid errors" | 资料抽取 |
