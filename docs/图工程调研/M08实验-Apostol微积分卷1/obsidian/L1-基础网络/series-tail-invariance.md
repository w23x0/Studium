# 级数增删有限项不改变敛散性
*invariance of convergence under omitting or adding finitely many terms*

## 定义/陈述

The two series $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}a_{k}$ and $\sum_{k=p}^{\infty}a_{k}$ both converge or both diverge: a finite number of terms may be omitted or added at the beginning of a series without affecting its convergence or divergence.
