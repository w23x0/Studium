# 渐近相等
*asymptotically equal*

## 定义/陈述

Two sequences $\{a_n\}$ and $\{b_n\}$ of complex numbers are said to be asymptotically equal if $\lim_{n \to \infty} a_n/b_n = 1$. It is intended to suggest that $a_n$ and $b_n$ behave in essentially the same way for large $n$.
