# 十进制展开的级数表示
*decimal expansion as a convergent series*

## 定义/陈述

Every positive real $x$ has a decimal representation $x = a_{0} \cdot a_{1} a_{2} a_{3} \dots$ with $0 \leq a_k \leq 9$ for $k \geq 1$, and x is given by the convergent series $x = \sum_ {k = 0} ^ {\infty} \frac {a _ {k}}{1 0 ^ {k}}$ (10.36), since $0 \leq x - s_n < 10^{-n}$. The representation generalizes by replacing 10 by any integer b > 1, giving an expansion in the scale of b (binary scale b = 2, duodecimal scale b = 12).
