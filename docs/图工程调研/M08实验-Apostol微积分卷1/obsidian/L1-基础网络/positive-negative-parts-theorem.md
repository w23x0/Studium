# 正部与负部级数的敛散性(定理 10.21)
*Divergence/convergence of positive and negative parts (Theorem 10.21)*

## 定义/陈述

Given a series $\sum a_n$ of real terms, define $a_n^+$ and $a_n^-$ by (10.59). (a) If $\sum a_n$ is conditionally convergent, both $\sum a_n^+$ and $\sum a_n^-$ diverge. (b) If $\sum a_n$ is absolutely convergent, both $\sum a_n^+$ and $\sum a_n^-$ converge, and $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n^+ + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n^-$ (10.60).
