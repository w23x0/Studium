# 复值数列收敛判别(实部虚部分别收敛)
*convergence criterion for complex-valued sequences*

## 定义/陈述

A complex-valued sequence f converges if and only if both the real part u and the imaginary part v converge separately, in which case $\lim _ {n \to \infty} f (n) = \lim _ {n \to \infty} u (n) + i \lim _ {n \to \infty} v (n)$.

> 适用语境:For $f = u + iv$ with real part u and imaginary part v; unnumbered result in Section 10.2
