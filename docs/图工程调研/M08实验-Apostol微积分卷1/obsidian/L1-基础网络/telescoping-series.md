# 裂项级数(套叠级数)
*telescoping series*

## 定义/陈述

Series $\sum a_{n}$ for which each term $a_{n}$ may be expressed as a difference of the form $a _ {n} = b _ {n} - b _ {n + 1}$ (10.22) are known as telescoping series. Note: every series is telescoping, since (10.22) can always be satisfied by choosing $b_{1}$ arbitrary and $b_{n+1} = b_{1} - s_{n}$.
