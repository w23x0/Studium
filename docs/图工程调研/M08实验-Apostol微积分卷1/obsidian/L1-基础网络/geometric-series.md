# 几何级数(等比级数)
*geometric series*

## 定义/陈述

The series $\sum x^{n}$ generated by successive addition of the terms in a geometric progression, where the nth term $x^{n}$ is the nth power of a fixed real or complex number x; the series starts with n = 0, the initial term $x^{0}$ being equal to 1. Its partial sums satisfy $s _ {n} = \frac {1 - x ^ {n}}{1 - x}$ if $x \neq 1$.

> 适用语境:x a fixed real or complex number; convergence settled by Theorem 10.5
> 出处:`10.08.md`
