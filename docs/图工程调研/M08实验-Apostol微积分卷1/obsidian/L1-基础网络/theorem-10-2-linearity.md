# 定理 10.2(收敛级数的线性性质)
*Theorem 10.2 (linearity of convergent series)*

## 定义/陈述

THEOREM 10.2. Let $\sum a_{n}$ and $\sum b_{n}$ be convergent infinite series of complex terms and let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be complex constants. Then the series $\sum (\alpha a_{n} + \beta b_{n})$ also converges, and $\sum_ {n = 1} ^ {\infty} (\alpha a _ {n} + \beta b _ {n}) = \alpha \sum_ {n = 1} ^ {\infty} a _ {n} + \beta \sum_ {n = 1} ^ {\infty} b _ {n}$ (10.19).

> 适用语境:Combines the additive property (10.17) and homogeneous property (10.18) of finite sums, extended to convergent series
