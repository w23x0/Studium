# 幂级数
*power series*

## 定义/陈述

Series of the special form $\sum_ {n = 0} ^ {\infty} a _ {n} x ^ {n}$ are known as power series. The numbers $a_{0}, a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots$, which may be real or complex, are called coefficients of the power series. If x and all the coefficients are real, the series is called a real power series.
