# 收敛级数及其和
*convergent series and its sum*

## 定义/陈述

If there is a real or complex number $S$ such that $\lim _ {n \to \infty} s _ {n} = S$, we say that the series $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} a_k$ is convergent and has the sum $S$, in which case we write $\sum_ {k = 1} ^ {\infty} a _ {k} = S$. The sum is not obtained by ordinary addition but as the limit of the sequence of partial sums.
