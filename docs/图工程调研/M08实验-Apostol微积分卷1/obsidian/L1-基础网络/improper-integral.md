# 瑕积分(广义积分)
*Improper integral*

## 定义/陈述

Extensions of the integral $\int_{a}^{b}f(x)dx$ beyond bounded functions on finite intervals: studying the behavior as $b\to+\infty$ leads to improper integrals of the first kind (infinite integrals), while keeping $[a,b]$ finite and allowing $f$ to become unbounded at one or more points leads (by a suitable limit process) to improper integrals of the second kind.

> 适用语境:Umbrella concept covering both kinds; contrasted with the proper integrals of Chapter 1.
