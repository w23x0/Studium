# 条件收敛级数
*Conditionally convergent series*

## 定义/陈述

A series $\sum a_n$ is called conditionally convergent if $\sum a_n$ converges but $\sum |a_n|$ diverges.

> 适用语境:实项或复项级数;实项条件收敛级数含无穷多正项与无穷多负项(10.21 节)
> 出处:`10.18.md`
