# 正整数集的置换
*Permutation of P*

## 定义/陈述

Let $\mathbf{P} = \{1, 2, 3, \ldots\}$ denote the set of positive integers. A function $f$ with domain $\mathbf{P}$ and range $\mathbf{P}$ such that $m \neq n$ implies $f(m) \neq f(n)$ is called a permutation of $\mathbf{P}$, or a one-to-one mapping of $\mathbf{P}$ onto itself.
