# 定理 10.3(收敛级数与发散级数之和发散)
*Theorem 10.3 (convergent plus divergent series diverges)*

## 定义/陈述

THEOREM 10.3. If $\sum a_{n}$ converges and if $\sum b_{n}$ diverges, then $\sum (a_{n} + b_{n})$ diverges.

> 适用语境:Corollary of Theorem 10.2, often used to establish divergence; if both series diverge, the sum may or may not converge
