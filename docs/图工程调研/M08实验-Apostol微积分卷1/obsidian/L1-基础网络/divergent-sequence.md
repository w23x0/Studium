# 发散数列
*divergent sequence*

## 定义/陈述

A sequence which does not converge is called divergent. A sequence with an infinite limit is said to diverge; there are also divergent sequences that do not have infinite limits, e.g. $f(n) = (-1)^n$.

> 适用语境:Includes sequences with infinite limits and oscillating sequences
