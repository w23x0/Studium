# 芝诺赛道悖论
*Zeno's racecourse paradox*

## 定义/陈述

A runner can never reach the end of a racecourse because he must cover half of any distance before he covers the whole. Formulated with time intervals, it leads to the infinite series $T + \frac {T}{2} + \frac {T}{4} + \dots + \frac {T}{2 ^ {n}} + \dots$ (10.1), whose partial sums $s_n = (2 - 1/2^{n-1})T$ tend to $2T$; with the modified speeds it leads to $T + \frac {T}{2} + \frac {T}{3} + \dots + \frac {T}{n} + \dots$ (10.5), whose partial sums grow without bound.
