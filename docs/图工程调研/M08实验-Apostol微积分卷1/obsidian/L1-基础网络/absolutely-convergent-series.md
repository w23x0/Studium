# 绝对收敛级数
*Absolutely convergent series*

## 定义/陈述

A series $\sum a_n$ is called absolutely convergent if $\sum |a_n|$ converges.

> 适用语境:实项或复项级数;若 $\sum a_n$ 与 $\sum b_n$ 绝对收敛,则 $\sum(\alpha a_n+\beta b_n)$ 亦然
