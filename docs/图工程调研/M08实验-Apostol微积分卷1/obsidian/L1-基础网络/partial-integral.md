# 部分积分(部分和的类比)
*Partial integrals*

## 定义/陈述

The function values $I(b)=\int_{a}^{b}f(x)dx$ play the role of the "partial sums" of a series and may be referred to as "partial integrals."

> 适用语境:Terminology for the finite integrals underlying an infinite integral, in analogy with partial sums of infinite series.
