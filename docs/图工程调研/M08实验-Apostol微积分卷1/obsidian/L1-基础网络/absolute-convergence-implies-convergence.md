# 绝对收敛蕴涵收敛(定理 10.15)
*Absolute convergence implies convergence (Theorem 10.15)*

## 定义/陈述

Assume $\sum |a_n|$ converges. Then $\sum a_n$ also converges, and we have $\left|\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n\right| \leq \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} |a_n|$ (10.51).
