# 积分的控制(优积分)
*Dominate (for integrals)*

## 定义/陈述

In the situation of Theorem 10.24 (with $0\leq f(x)\leq g(x)$ for all $x\geq a$), the integral $\int_{a}^{\infty}g(x)dx$ is said to dominate the integral $\int_{a}^{\infty}f(x)dx$.
