# 发散级数
*divergent series*

## 定义/陈述

If the sequence of partial sums $\{s_n\}$ diverges, we say that the series $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} a_k$ diverges and has no sum.

> 适用语境:Series of real or complex terms
