# 阿贝尔分部求和公式
*Abel's partial summation formula*

## 定义/陈述

Let $\{a_n\}$ and $\{b_n\}$ be two sequences of complex numbers, and let $A_n = \sum_{k=1}^{n} a_k$. Then $\sum_{k=1}^{n} a_k b_k = A_n b_{n+1} + \sum_{k=1}^{n} A_k (b_k - b_{k+1})$ (10.52). Analogous to integration by parts.
