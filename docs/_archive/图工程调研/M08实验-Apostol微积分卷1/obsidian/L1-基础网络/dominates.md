# 优超(级数间的控制关系)
*dominates*

## 定义/陈述

When the inequality $a_n \leq c b_n$ (10.38) is satisfied for some positive constant $c$, we say that the series $\sum b_n$ dominates the series $\sum a_n$.

> 适用语境:Series of nonnegative terms, in the context of the comparison test
