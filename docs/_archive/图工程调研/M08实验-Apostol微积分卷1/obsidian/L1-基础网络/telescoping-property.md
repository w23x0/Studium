# 有限和的裂项(套叠)性质
*telescoping property of finite sums*

## 定义/陈述

The telescoping property of finite sums states that $\sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} (b _ {k} - b _ {k + 1}) = b _ {1} - b _ {n + 1}$ (10.21).
