# 收敛判别法
*tests for convergence*

## 定义/陈述

Criteria that decide the convergence or divergence of a series $\sum a_n$ without explicit knowledge of its partial sums. They are broadly classified into three categories: (i) sufficient conditions ("If $C$ is satisfied, then $\sum a_n$ converges"); (ii) necessary conditions ("If $\sum a_n$ converges, then $C$ is satisfied"); (iii) necessary and sufficient conditions ("$\sum a_n$ converges if and only if $C$ is satisfied").

> 适用语境:General infinite series $\sum a_n$
