# 级数求和记号 Σ a_k
*series notation Σ a_k*

## 定义/陈述

The symbol $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}a_{k}$ denotes both the series and (when convergent) its sum, even though the two are conceptually distinct. The letter k is a dummy index and may be replaced by any other convenient symbol; summation may start from any $p \geq 0$, and $\sum a_{k}$ is written when the starting point is unimportant.
