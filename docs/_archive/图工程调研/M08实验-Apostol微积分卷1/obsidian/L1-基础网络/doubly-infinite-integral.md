# 双侧无穷积分的收敛
*Convergence of the integral from minus infinity to infinity*

## 定义/陈述

If $\int_{-\infty}^{c}f(x)dx$ and $\int_{c}^{\infty}f(x)dx$ are both convergent for some $c$, the integral $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x)dx$ is convergent, and its value is defined as $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x)dx=\int_{-\infty}^{c}f(x)dx+\int_{c}^{\infty}f(x)dx$ (10.62); the choice of $c$ is unimportant. The integral diverges if at least one of the integrals on the right of (10.62) is divergent.

> 适用语境:Infinite integrals over the entire real line, reduced to two one-sided infinite integrals.
