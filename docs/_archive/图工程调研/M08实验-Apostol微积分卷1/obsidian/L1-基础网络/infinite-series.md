# 无穷级数
*infinite series*

## 定义/陈述

The sequence $\{s_{n}\}$ of partial sums of a given sequence $\{a_{n}\}$ is called an infinite series, or simply a series, and is also denoted by $a _ {1} + a _ {2} + a _ {3} + \dots$ or $\sum_ {k = 1} ^ {\infty} a _ {k}$ (10.15).
