# 交错级数
*Alternating series*

## 定义/陈述

Series whose terms alternate in sign, of the form $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (-1)^{n-1} a_n = a_1 - a_2 + a_3 - a_4 + \dots + (-1)^{n-1} a_n + \dots$, where each $a_n > 0$.
