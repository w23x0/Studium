# 二项式定理
*binomial theorem*

## 定义/陈述

$(1 + x) ^ {n} = \sum_ {k = 0} ^ {n} {\binom {n} {k}} x ^ {k}$, where x is an arbitrary real number, n is a nonnegative integer, and $\binom{n}{k}$ is the binomial coefficient. Newton extended the formula to arbitrary real exponents by replacing the finite sum with a suitable infinite series (the binomial series).
