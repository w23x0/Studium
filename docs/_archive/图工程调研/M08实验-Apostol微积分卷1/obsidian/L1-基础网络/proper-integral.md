# 常义积分(正常积分)
*Proper integral*

## 定义/陈述

The integrals of Chapter 1, defined for a function $f$ that is defined and bounded on a finite interval $[a,b]$; the term "proper" distinguishes them from improper integrals.

> 适用语境:Terminology introduced to contrast with improper integrals.
> 出处:`10.23.md`

## 原文锚点

- `10.23.md`:“To distinguish the integrals of Chapter 1 from improper integrals, the former are often called “proper” integrals.”
