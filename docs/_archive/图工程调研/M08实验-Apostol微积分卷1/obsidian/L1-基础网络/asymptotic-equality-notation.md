# 渐近相等记号 $a_n \sim b_n$
*the notation $a_n \sim b_n$*

## 定义/陈述

The relation of asymptotic equality is indicated symbolically by writing $a_n \sim b_n$ as $n \to \infty$ (10.40), read "$a_n$ is asymptotically equal to $b_n$".

> 适用语境:Shorthand for $\lim_{n \to \infty} a_n/b_n = 1$
