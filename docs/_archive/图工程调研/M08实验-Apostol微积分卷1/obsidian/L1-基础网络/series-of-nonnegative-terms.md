# 非负项级数
*series of nonnegative terms*

## 定义/陈述

A series of the form $\sum a_n$, where each $a_n \geq 0$. The partial sums of such a series form a monotonic increasing sequence.

> 适用语境:Real series; basis for the comparison, integral, root and ratio tests
