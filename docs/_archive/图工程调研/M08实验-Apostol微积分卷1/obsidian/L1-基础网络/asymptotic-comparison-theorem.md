# 定理10.10(渐近相等的级数同敛散)
*Theorem 10.10 (asymptotically equal series converge or diverge together)*

## 定义/陈述

Two series $\sum a_n$ and $\sum b_n$ with terms that are positive and asymptotically equal converge together or they diverge together.

> 适用语境:Series of positive terms; restatement of the limit comparison test in asymptotic-equality language
