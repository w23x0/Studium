# 数列的极限
*limit of a sequence*

## 定义/陈述

DEFINITION. A sequence $\{f(n)\}$ is said to have a limit L if, for every positive number $\epsilon$, there is another positive number N (which may depend on $\epsilon$) such that $| f (n) - L | < \epsilon$ for all $n \geq N$. In this case we write $\lim _ {n \rightarrow \infty} f (n) = L$, or $f (n) \rightarrow L$ as $n \rightarrow \infty$.

> 适用语境:The function values $f(n)$ and the limit $L$ may be real or complex numbers
