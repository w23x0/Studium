# 阿贝尔判别法
*Abel's test*

## 定义/陈述

Let $\sum a_n$ be a convergent series of complex terms and let $\{b_n\}$ be a monotonic convergent sequence of real terms. Then the series $\sum a_n b_n$ converges.

> 适用语境:$\sum a_n$ 收敛(复项),$\{b_n\}$ 单调收敛(实项)
