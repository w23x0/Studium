# 定理10.7(非负项级数收敛的有界性准则)
*Theorem 10.7 (boundedness criterion for nonnegative series)*

## 定义/陈述

Assume that $a_n \geq 0$ for each $n \geq 1$. Then the series $\sum a_n$ converges if and only if the sequence of its partial sums is bounded above. Moreover, if the partial sums are bounded above by a number $M$, then the sum of the series cannot exceed $M$.
