# 数列记号 {f(n)}
*sequence notation {f(n)}*

## 定义/陈述

The notation $\{f(n)\}$ denotes the sequence whose nth term is $f(n)$. Very often the dependence on n is denoted by subscripts, writing $a_{n}, s_{n}, x_{n}, u_{n}$ instead of $f(n)$.

> 适用语境:Used throughout Chapter 10 for sequences with real or complex terms
