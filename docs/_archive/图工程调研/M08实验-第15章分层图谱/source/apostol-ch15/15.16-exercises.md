6. In the real linear space $C(1,3)$ with inner product $(f,g) = \int_{1}^{3} f(x)g(x) dx$ , let $f(x) = 1 / x$ and show that the constant polynomial $g$ nearest to $f$ is $g = \frac{1}{2} \log 3$ . Compute $\| g - f \|^2$ for this $g$ .

7. In the real linear space $C(0,2)$ with inner product $(f,g) = \int_0^2 f(x)g(x)dx$ , let $f(x) = e^x$ and show that the constant polynomial $g$ nearest to $f$ is $g = \frac{1}{2}(e^2 - 1)$ . Compute $\| g - f \|^2$ for this $g$ .

8. In the real linear space $C(-1,1)$ with inner product $(f,g) = \int_{-1}^{1} f(x)g(x) dx$ , let $f(x) = e^x$ and find the linear polynomial $g$ nearest to $f$ . Compute $\| g - f \|^2$ for this $g$ .
