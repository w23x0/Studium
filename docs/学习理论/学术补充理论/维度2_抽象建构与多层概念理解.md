## 2.1 概念压缩理论 Procept / Encapsulation

过程→对象的认知跃迁，低层操作变为高层可操作对象。

## 2.2 APOS 理论 Action–Process–Object–Schema

数学概念建构四阶段：动作→过程→对象→图式。与 2.1 同属 Dubinsky 学派，强关联。

### 核心文献 A — 理论命名与综述
- Dubinsky, Ed & McDonald, Michael A. (2001). *APOS: A Constructivist Theory of Learning in Undergraduate Mathematics Education Research*. In D. Holton (Ed.), *The Teaching and Learning of Mathematics at University Level: An ICMI Study* (New ICMI Study Series, Vol. 7, 通常引作 pp. 275–282，需核实). Kluwer Academic Publishers.
- DOI（章节）：10.1007/0-306-47231-7_25
- 链接：
  - Springer 章节页：https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/0-306-47231-7_25
  - 整书页：https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/0-306-47231-7
  - ResearchGate（有 PDF）：https://www.researchgate.net/publication/227004665_APOS_A_Constructivist_Theory_of_Learning_in_Undergraduate_Mathematics_Education_Research

### 核心文献 B — 奠基性研究框架（APOS 方法论源头）
- Asiala, M., Brown, A., DeVries, D. J., Dubinsky, E., Mathews, D. & Thomas, K. (1996). *A Framework for Research and Curriculum Development in Undergraduate Mathematics Education*. *Research in Collegiate Mathematics Education II* (CBMS Issues in Mathematics Education, Vol. 6, pp. 1–32). American Mathematical Society.
- DOI：**需进一步核实**（AMS CBMS 早期卷常无 DOI）
- 链接：
  - AMS 卷目录：https://www.ams.org/books/cbmath/006/
  - CAUSEweb 条目：https://causeweb.org/cause/research/literature/framework-research-and-curriculum-development-undergraduate-mathematics

补充：APOS 缩写最早见于 Cottrill et al.（1996），思想可溯至 Dubinsky（1984）。

## 2.3 阈值概念理论 Threshold Concepts

## 2.4 表征转换理论 Representational Transfer / Registers of Semiotic Representation

在符号/图形/语言等多种表征间灵活转换的能力。两条不同传统：英美"表征间转换"与 Duval 的法国"符号表征语域"。

### 核心文献 A — Duval（符号表征语域；treatment & conversion）
- Duval, Raymond (2006). *A Cognitive Analysis of Problems of Comprehension in a Learning of Mathematics*. *Educational Studies in Mathematics*, 61(1–2), 103–131.
- DOI：**10.1007/s10649-006-0400-z**
- 链接：
  - Springer PDF：https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s10649-006-0400-z.pdf

  - ERIC：https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ748122

- DOI：无
