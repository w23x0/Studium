## 4.1 自我调节学习 Self-Regulated Learning (SRL)

计划→监控→调节→反思的元认知循环。两条均为 *Handbook of Self-Regulation* (Boekaerts, Pintrich & Zeidner Eds., 2000, Academic Press, ISBN 978-0-12-109890-2) 中的章节。

### 核心文献 A — Zimmerman 章节
- Zimmerman, Barry J. (2000). *Attaining self-regulation: A social cognitive perspective*. In *Handbook of Self-Regulation* (pp. 13–39). San Diego: Academic Press.
- DOI（章节）：10.1016/B978-012109890-2/50031-7（**需核实**，ScienceDirect 章节通用格式）
- 链接：
  - ResearchGate：https://www.researchgate.net/publication/277681069_Attaining_Self-Regulation
  - 全书（Elsevier）：https://business.elsevier.com/handbook-of-self-regulation-978-0-12-109890-2-html.html

### 核心文献 B — Pintrich 章节
- Pintrich, Paul R. (2000). *The role of goal orientation in self-regulated learning*. In *Handbook of Self-Regulation* (pp. 451–502). San Diego: Academic Press.
- DOI（章节）：10.1016/B978-012109890-2/50043-3（**需核实**）
- 链接：
  - SciSpace：https://typeset.io/papers/the-role-of-goal-orientation-in-self-regulated-learning-49wxpl56vj

## 4.4 延迟满足与目标梯度 / 双曲贴现 Delay of Gratification / Goal Gradient / Hyperbolic Discounting
