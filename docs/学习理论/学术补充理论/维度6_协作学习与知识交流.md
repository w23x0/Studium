## 6.2 自我解释效应 Self-Explanation Effect

向他人（或自己）解释时理解会深化重构。两篇不同论文。**这条对单人自学也强适用（自问自答式解释）。**

### 6.2a Chi et al. (1989)
- Chi, Michelene T. H.; Bassok, Miriam; Lewis, Matthew W.; Reimann, Peter & Glaser, Robert (1989). *Self-explanations: How students study and use examples in learning to solve problems*. *Cognitive Science*, 13(2), 145–182.
- DOI：**10.1207/s15516709cog1302_1**
- 链接：
  - Wiley：https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1207/s15516709cog1302_1
  - ASU 作者主页：https://education.asu.edu/lcl/publications/chi-m-t-h-bassok-m-lewis-m-reimann-p-glaser-r-1989-self-explanations-how-students
  - DTIC 技术报告版（可下载）：https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/citations/ADA187035

### 6.2b Chi et al. (1994)
- Chi, Michelene T. H.; de Leeuw, Nicholas; Chiu, Mei-Hung & LaVancher, Christian (1994). *Eliciting self-explanations improves understanding*. *Cognitive Science*, 18(3), 439–477.
- DOI：**10.1207/s15516709cog1803_3**
- 链接：
  - Wiley：https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1207/s15516709cog1803_3
  - ASU 作者主页：https://education.asu.edu/lcl/publications/chi-m-t-h-de-leeuw-n-chiu-m-h-lavancher-c-1994-eliciting-self-explanations-0

## 6.3 最近发展区 Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD)

通过与更强者互动突破个体学习天花板。

### 核心文献
- Vygotsky, Lev S. (1978). *Mind in Society: The Development of Higher Psychological Processes* (Cole, M., John-Steiner, V., Scribner, S. & Souberman, E., Eds.). Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press.（ISBN 9780674576292）
- 注：Vygotsky 1934 年逝，本书为其文稿英文编译本。ZPD 核心在第 6 章 "Interaction Between Learning and Development"。
- 链接：
  - Harvard University Press：https://www.hup.harvard.edu/books/9780674576292
  - HUP 目次：https://www.hup.harvard.edu/catalog.php?content=toc&isbn=9780674576292
  - archive.org（可借阅全文）：https://archive.org/details/mindinsocietydev00vygo

### 关联
- → 已有模块 [[信息差与外部资源利用]]（导师/学长=更强他者）、[[学习者效能理论]]

## 6.4 学术写作作为思维工具 Writing as a Thinking Tool

写作不只是表达思维，写作本身就是思维过程。**对单人自学直接适用。**

### 核心文献
- Flower, Linda & Hayes, John R. (1981). *A cognitive process theory of writing*. *College Composition and Communication*, 32(4), 365–387.
- DOI：**10.2307/356600**（Crossref 实际解析确认）
