# Snapshot: MaRDI Bravo (arXiv:2309.11484)

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.11484
Fetched: 2026-08-14

## Abstract (verbatim excerpts)

"no expertly curated knowledge graph has focused specifically on contemporary mathematics."

"comprehensive knowledge graph that links multimodal research data in mathematics. This encompasses traditional research data items like datasets, software, and publications and includes semantically advanced objects such as mathematical formulas and hypotheses."

"based on Wikibase"

"We have seeded the MaRDI KG with more than 100 million triples aggregated from various sources."

## Relevance note

MaRDI models formulas, hypotheses, datasets, software, publications as linked Wikibase entities — granularity is **object-type rich**, vocabulary is **community-extensible LOD**, not a closed relation enum.
