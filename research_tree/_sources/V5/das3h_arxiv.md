## 内容

Title: "DAS3H: Modeling Student Learning and Forgetting for Optimally Scheduling Distributed Practice of Skills"
Authors: Benoît Choffin, Fabrice Popineau, Yolaine Bourda, Jill-Jênn Vie
arXiv: 1905.06873v1 (2019-05-14)，EDM 2019（第 12 届教育数据挖掘国际会议）

核心：DAS3H "builds on the additive factor models and includes a representation of the temporal distribution of past practice on the skills"——在加法因子模型（AFM）之上加入对技能练习时间分布的建模（即遗忘/间隔维度）。

证据：DAS3H "outperforms other state-of-the-art EDM models" across three educational datasets.

注：该模型把"遗忘"建模为距离上次练习的时间函数，把"间隔"建模为练习的时间分布特征，用于优化分布式练习的调度。
