## 核心内容

- 声明性知识以 chunk 形式："declarative knowledge is represented in the form of chunks, i.e. vector representations of individual properties, each of them accessible from a labelled slot."
- 模块化：chunks 放在 modules 中，经 buffers 访问："the front-end of what are modules, i.e. specialized and largely independent brain structures."
- 两个记忆模块：declarative memory "consisting of facts such as Washington, D.C. is the capital of United States"；procedural memory "made of productions."
- 建模效应："the fan effect of interference for associated information," "primacy and recency effects for list memory," "serial recall."
- 理性分析（Rational Analysis）："The basic assumption of Rational Analysis is that cognition is optimally adaptive, and precise estimates of cognitive functions mirror statistical properties of the environment." 支撑激活计算。
- 应用：运行模型 "automatically produces a step-by-step simulation of human behavior which specifies each individual cognitive operation"，每步含 "quantitative predictions of latencies and accuracies"；可预测 BOLD 响应。
- 注：基级学习/激活方程（如 B_i = ln Σ t_j^-d）未在此页复现，需 Anderson & Lebiere 1998 / Anderson 2007。
