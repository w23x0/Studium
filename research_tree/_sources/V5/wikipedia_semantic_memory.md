## 核心内容

- 定义："Semantic memory refers to general world knowledge that humans have accumulated throughout their lives."
- Tulving (1972) 区分语义/情景记忆：语义=通用、与上下文无关的事实；情景=具体个人经验。"semantic memory might contain information about what a cat is, whereas episodic memory might contain a specific memory of stroking a particular cat."
- Jacoby & Dallas (1981)：实验分离证据。
- 词联想法：Word Association Spaces (WAS, Steyvers, Shiffrin, Nelson)，"words that have similar associative structures are placed in similar regions of space." Latent Semantic Analysis (LSA) 用词共现测语义相关。
- Collins & Quillian 的 Teachable Language Comprehender (TLC)：第一个网络模型之一。"each node is a word, representing a concept"；属性存在最高可应用类别层；加工用扩散激活，"the time to answer the question 'Is a chicken a bird?' is a function of how far the activation between the nodes" must spread。原模型缺加权链接，"failed to predict that people would respond faster to questions regarding more typical category instances." Collins & Loftus 后用加权连接修正（熟悉性与典型性效应）。
- 网络组织："its links are almost always directed" and "the links come in many different types." 分布式而非纯层级：Thompson-Schill (2003) 颜色/形状/运动/大小知识映射不同皮层区。Lambon Ralph, Lowe, & Rogers (2007)：语义性痴呆 vs 疱疹性脑炎的不同损伤模式。
- 知识独立于情境："Semantic memory's contents are not tied to any particular instance of experience"，存的是 "the 'gist' of experience, an abstract structure that applies to a wide variety of experiential objects."
