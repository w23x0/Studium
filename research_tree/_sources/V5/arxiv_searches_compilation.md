## 1. Measuring individual semantic networks: A simulation study
- Authors: Samuel Aeschbach, Rui Mata, Dirk U. Wulff
- arXiv: 2410.18326v1, 2024
- 摘要："Accurately capturing individual differences in semantic networks is fundamental to advancing our mechanistic understanding of semantic memory. Past empirical attempts to construct individual-level semantic networks from behavioral paradigms may be limited by data constraints... our results show that successful inference of semantic networks is achievable, but they also highlight critical challenges. Estimates of absolute network characteristics are severely biased, such that comparisons between behavioral paradigms and different design configurations are often not meaningful. However, comparisons within a given paradigm and design configuration can be accurate and generalizable when based on designs with moderate numbers of cues, moderate numbers of responses, and cue sets including diverse words."

## 2. Sparsey: Event Recognition via Deep Hierarchical Sparse Distributed Codes
- Author: Gerard J. Rinkus
- arXiv: 1611.04023v1, 2016（发表于 Frontiers in Computational Neuroscience, 2014）
- 要点：Sparsey 用稀疏分布编码(SDC)于所有层级 macrocolumn；SDC 允许 "storing a new item and retrieving the best-matching stored item, to be done in fixed time for the life of the model."

## 3. Superposed Episodic and Semantic Memory via Sparse Distributed Representation
- Authors: Rod Rinkus, Jasmin Leveillé
- arXiv: 1710.07829v1, 2017
- 要点：用 Sparsey 的 SDR 使情景记忆与语义记忆共存；单次学习将情景痕迹叠加存储；作为副产品，生成式模型(语义记忆) "computationally free" 地浮现（输入相似性经交集映射为码空间相似性）。

## 4. Sparse Distributed Representation, Hierarchy, Critical Periods, Metaplasticity
- Author: Gerard Rinkus
- arXiv: 1806.04704v1, 2018
- 要点：Sparsey 四个特征：单次/少次在线学习、永久的记忆（免灾难性遗忘）、终身常数时间学习与检索、永不满。

## 5. 其他（间隔重复相关）
- Adaptive Forgetting Curves for Spaced Repetition Language Learning（Zaidi et al., arXiv:2004.11327, 2020）：遗忘曲线模型+心理学/语言特征预测单词回忆概率；"word complexity is a highly informative feature."
- Unbounded Human Learning: Optimal Scheduling for Spaced Repetition（Reddy, Labutov, Banerjee, Joachims, arXiv:1602.07032, 2016）：队列网络模型 + "a sharp phase transition in learning outcomes upon increasing the rate of new item introductions."
- Spaced Repetition and Mnemonics Enable Recall of Multiple Strong Passwords（Blocki, Komanduri, Cranor, Datta, arXiv:1410.1490, 2014）：最佳计划（初始 12 小时回归，1.5× 递增间隔）158 天内 77% 回忆率。
- From semantic memory to collective creativity（Ahmed & Baten, arXiv:2602.03068, 2026）：多主体模型，agents 共享语义词汇但 "differ in semantic network topology"，单一生成参数调节语义模块度，产生 "emergent individual differences in ideational breadth."
