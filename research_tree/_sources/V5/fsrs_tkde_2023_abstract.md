Title: Optimizing Spaced Repetition Schedule by Capturing the Dynamics of Memory
Authors: Jingyong Su, Junyao Ye, Liqiang Nie, Yilong Cao, Yongyong Chen
Year: 2023. Venue: IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering. DOI: 10.1109/TKDE.2023.3251721.

摘要摘录：
- "Spaced repetition, namely, learners review items in a given schedule, has been proven powerful for memorization and practice of skills."
- "Most current spaced repetition methods focus on either predicting student recall or designing an optimal review schedule"
- "we propose a novel spaced repetition schedule framework by capturing the dynamics of memory, which alternates memory prediction"
- "the spaced repetition optimization is transformed a stochastic shortest path problem and solved via the value iteration method"
- "the proposed approach reduces 64% error and 17% cost in predicting recall rates and optimizing schedules compared to several baselines."
