Title: Homotopy Type Theory: Univalent Foundations of Mathematics
Authors: The Univalent Foundations Program
Publisher: self-published (IAS), first edition 2013

Key text (verbatim):
- HoTT is "a new branch of mathematics that combines aspects of several different fields in a surprising way"
- "based on a recently discovered connection between homotopy theory and type theory"
- "a first systematic exposition of the basics of univalent foundations"
- "a central role ... played by Voevodsky's univalence axiom and higher inductive types"
- "We believe that univalent foundations will eventually become a viable alternative to set theory as the 'implicit foundation' for the unformalized mathematics done by most mathematicians."
- touches "the homotopy groups of spheres, the algorithms for type checking, and the definition of weak ∞-groupoids"
- "the homotopy notion of equalities as paths"
- Free PDFs under permissive Creative Commons license.

背景（《几个方向》）: univalence = Voevodsky 2006年前后提出; 2012-13 IAS 专题年; 2013 HoTT 书出版。
