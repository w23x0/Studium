# Knowledge Graph Embedding: An Overview (arXiv:2309.12501)
抓取日期：2026-08-13 | URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.12501 | A 级

Title: Knowledge Graph Embedding: An Overview
Authors: Xiou Ge, Yun-Cheng Wang, Bin Wang, C.-C. Jay Kuo
Submitted: 21 Sep 2023 (v1)

Abstract (verbatim, key sentences):
- "Many mathematical models have been leveraged to design embeddings for representing Knowledge Graph (KG) entities and relations" for link prediction and downstream tasks.
- models are "highly scalable for inference in large KGs" and have "explainable advantages in modeling different relation patterns"
- survey focuses on "two main branches of KG embedding (KGE) design: 1) distance-based methods and 2) semantic matching-based methods"
- discusses CompoundE/CompoundE3D "draw inspiration from 2D and 3D affine operations"
- "an emerging approach" using "pre-trained language models (PLMs) and textual descriptions"
- aims to "discover the connections between recently proposed models and present an underlying trend that might help researchers invent novel and more effective models"

→ 把 KG 的实体/关系投影为低维向量（嵌入），用于链接预测等；嵌入是对图谱的派生态，不是身份本体。
