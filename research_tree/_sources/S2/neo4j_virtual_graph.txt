"Virtual Graph (Public Preview) - Neo4j Aura"
"Virtual Graph is embedded in the Neo4j infrastructure: It is part of Neo4j Aura. It neither moves nor extracts data from your data lakehouse.
It has full access to other Neo4j products and features such as Browser, Bloom, GDS, and Copilot.
Virtualization makes the data found in data lakehouses immediately accessible. ... Virtual Graph works in three main steps:
Connect to your data lakehouse. Create a graph schema based on the tables found in the data.
Translate Cypher to optimized SQL queries and receive query results as graph data."
Industry precedent for a graph query layer over existing data without copying it (cloud/Aura only, public preview).
