SNAPSHOT: arXiv abs/1811.12640 — Inferring Concept Prerequisite Relations (PREREQ) URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.12640 Fetched: 2026-08-13 via WebFetch Paper: Sudeshna Roy, Meghana Madhyastha, Sheril Lawrence, Vaibhav Rajan. Accepted at IAAI-19. --- [Abstract, verbatim segments] "The Internet has rich and rapidly increasing sources of high quality educational content." "Inferring prerequisite relations between educational concepts is required for modern large-scale online educational technology" "applications such as personalized recommendations and automatic curriculum creation." "We present PREREQ, a new supervised learning method for inferring concept prerequisite relations." "PREREQ is designed using latent representations of concepts obtained from the Pairwise Latent Dirichlet Allocation model" "and a neural network based on the Siamese network architecture." "PREREQ can learn unknown concept prerequisites from course prerequisites and labeled concept prerequisite data." "It outperforms state-of-the-art approaches on benchmark datasets and can effectively learn from very less training data." "PREREQ can also use unlabeled video playlists, a steadily growing source of training data, to learn concept prerequisites" "thus obviating the need for manual annotation of course prerequisites." [Key claims] - Model: PREREQ = Pairwise LDA latent concept representations + Siamese NN for supervised prerequisite inference. - Learns from course prerequisites + labeled concept prerequisites; also unlabeled video playlists. - Outperforms state-of-the-art on benchmark datasets; works with little training data.
