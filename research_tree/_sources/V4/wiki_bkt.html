It models student knowledge in a hidden Markov model as a latent variable, updated by observing the correctness of each student's interaction in which they apply the skill in question." ## 二元变量假设（逐字） "BKT assumes that student knowledge is represented as a set of binary variables, one per skill, where the skill is either mastered by the student or not. Observations in BKT are also binary: a student gets a problem/step either right or wrong." ## 逐技能参数（逐字） "In its most common implementation, BKT has only skill-specific parameters." ## 四参数（Method 节，逐字；页面上参数名以 LaTeX 渲染为 p-init/p-transit/p-slip/p-guess） 1. "p(L0) or p-init, the probability of the student knowing the skill beforehand." 2. "p(T) or p-transit, the probability of the student demonstrating knowledge of the skill after an opportunity to apply it" 3. "p(S) or p-slip, the probability the student makes a mistake when applying a known skill" 4. "p(G) or p-guess, the probability that the student correctly applies an unknown skill (has a lucky guess)" ## 更新公式（Method 节，页面逐字；公式 a–e，LaTeX 渲染） - Equation (a): p(L1)_u^k = p(L0)^k（初始掌握概率 = 该技能 p-init） - Equation (b): p(L_t | obs=correct)_u^k = p(L_t)_u^k·(1−p(S)^k) / (p(L_t)_u^k·(1−p(S)^k) + (1−p(L_t)_u^k)·p(G)^k) - Equation (c): p(L_t | obs=wrong)_u^k = p(L_t)_u^k·p(S)^k / (p(L_t)_u^k·p(S)^k + (1−p(L_t)_u^k)·(1−p(G)^k)) - Equation (d): p(L_{t+1})_u^k = p(L_t|obs)_u^k + (1−p(L_t|obs)_u^k)·p(T)^k - Equation (e): p(C_{t+1})_u^k = p(L_{t+1})_u^k·(1−p(S)^k) + (1−p(L_{t+1})_u^k)·p(G)^k ## See also（逐字列出） Computerized adaptive testing / Item response theory / Knowledge space theory / Latent growth modeling ## References（页面参考文献节） - Corbett, A.
