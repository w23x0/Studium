Copyright ©2026 McGraw Hill. --- [Extracted content] ## What ALEKS Is ALEKS is an AI-driven learning and assessment platform for Math, Chemistry, Statistics, and Accounting, used by over 50 million students. Product associated with McGraw Hill and ALEKS Corporation (ALEKS is a registered trademark of ALEKS Corporation). The system has been developed over 26 years using billions of student data points. ## How It Works ALEKS first determines each student's precise knowledge of a subject, then guides them to topics they are ready to learn. It uses machine learning trained on massive datasets: "ALEKS artificial intelligence is developed using billions of data points from student interactions accumulated over 26 years." The system continuously maps what a student knows: "ALEKS knows, at each moment, with respect to each individual topic in the course, whether the individual student has mastered that topic." Students always work "at the boundaries of their current knowledge," so they avoid frustration or boredom. Progress is visualized as filling a "pie" divided into subject-area slices. ## Knowledge Space Theory ALEKS is explicitly grounded in Knowledge Space Theory. "ALEKS is Driven by Sophisticated Mathematical Cognitive Science: Knowledge Space Theory." On positioning learners: "ALEKS has developed advanced software that accurately places the student within a space of trillions of feasible knowledge states." The theory's academic credibility: "Hundreds of academic papers on Knowledge Space Theory have been published in peer-reviewed journals." ## Assessment & Knowledge Checks ALEKS determines a student's state through adaptive questioning: "based on their answers to a small number of questions chosen by ALEKS based on their responses to all previous questions." Periodic "Knowledge Checks" reinforce retention: "ALEKS Knowledge Checks automatically confirm and enhance retention, creating a continuous cycle of learning and assessment." Predictive accuracy: when ALEKS says a student is ready to learn a concept, "the student is able to master it at least 90% of the time," and in many courses that rate exceeds 95%. ## Affiliation & Dates ALEKS is a registered trademark of ALEKS Corporation, copyright held by McGraw Hill (©2026).
