[Extracted from abstract] - Adaptive assessment procedures "fundamentally differ from other current ones (such as those of S.A.T. and A.C.T.)." The approach "relies on a combinatorial viewpoint on the assessment of knowledge, and thus departs from common, numerical evaluation." - Knowledge states: "uncovering the knowledge states of individuals" via Markovian processes. - Learning Space Theory is the "special case" of Knowledge Space Theory; learning spaces are "huge combinatorial structures which may be difficult to manage." - ALEKS: "the ALEKS system, which includes a teaching component"; methods "have been used by millions of students in schools and colleges." - Abstract covers two "basic axioms," the "probabilistic part of the theory," and "Markovian type processes." [Relevance] Originator-authored formal statement that KST is combinatorial (set-subset knowledge states) and probabilistic (Markov), vs. numerical scoring (SAT/ACT).
