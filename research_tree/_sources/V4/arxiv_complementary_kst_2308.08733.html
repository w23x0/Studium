SNAPSHOT: arXiv abs/2308.08733 — A note on complementary knowledge spaces URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08733 Fetched: 2026-08-13 via WebFetch Author: Fucai Lin --- [Abstract, verbatim] "The pair (Q, K) is a knowledge space if ∪K = Q and K is closed under union, where Q is a nonempty set and K is a family of subsets of Q." A knowledge space is "called complementary if there exists a non-discrete knowledge space (Q, L)" satisfying: (i) Any singleton {q} can be expressed as a finite intersection of sets from both K and L: "(∩_{i=1}^n K_i) ∩ (∩_{j=1}^m L_j) = {q}" (ii) "K ∩ L = {∅, Q}" [Results] 1. Existence: "the existence of a complementary knowledge space for each knowledge space is proved." 2. Finite construction: "a method of the construction of complementary finite knowledge spaces is given." [Relevance] Formal definition of knowledge space: (Q,K) with ∪K = Q and K closed under union — i.e., KST's central structure is union-closed (a pre-topology), the set-dual of an intersection-closed closure system.
