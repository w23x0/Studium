Key claimed advantages: "The RNN family of models have important advantages over previous methods in that they do not require the explicit encoding of human domain knowledge, and can capture more complex representations of student knowledge." ## Results "Using neural networks results in substantial improvements in prediction performance on a range of knowledge tracing datasets." The model can be used "for intelligent curriculum design and allows straightforward interpretation and discovery of structure in student tasks." ## Comparison to BKT The abstract references "previous methods" that require "explicit encoding of human domain knowledge" — an allusion to BKT-style approaches. No specific AUC/accuracy numbers on the abstract page (in full PDF). ## Limitations No explicit limitations stated on the abstract page.
