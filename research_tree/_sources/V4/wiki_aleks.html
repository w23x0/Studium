SNAPSHOT: Wikipedia — ALEKS URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ALEKS Fetched: 2026-08-13 via WebFetch --- [Extracted content] ## What it is ALEKS stands for "Assessment and Learning in Knowledge Spaces." Described as "an online tutoring and assessment program" covering "mathematics, chemistry, introductory statistics, and business." ## Knowledge Space Theory Grounded in the work of Doignon and Falmagne (1999). Rather than relying on numerical test scores, the system uses combinatorial mathematics from knowledge space theory: "ALEKS uses the theory of knowledge spaces to develop a combinatorial understanding of the set of topics a student does or doesn't understand." ## How it works Periodic assessments called "knowledge checks" determine what a student is ready to learn next. "Based on this assessment (called a knowledge check) determines the topics that the student is ready to learn and allows the student to choose from interactive learning modules." ## History and Ownership Development began at UC Irvine in 1994 with NSF funding. Technology licensed exclusively to ALEKS Corporation, acquired by McGraw-Hill Education in 2013. ## Limitations or Criticism The article does not explicitly state limitations or criticisms.
