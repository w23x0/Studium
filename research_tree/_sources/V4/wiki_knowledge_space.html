The article notes they "remain in extensive use in the education theory." A major modern application is ALEKS, a "computerized tutoring system" built on knowledge space theory. ## Knowledge State The model presumes an educational subject S can be modeled as a finite set Q of concepts, skills, or topics. "Each feasible state of knowledge about S is then a subset of Q." So a knowledge state is any subset of Q representing what a learner could feasibly know. ## Knowledge Structure A knowledge structure is the minimal formal object: "A knowledge structure assumes that K contains the empty set … and Q itself." In other words, K must include the state of knowing nothing and the state of knowing everything. ## Knowledge Space / Closure Under Union A knowledge space adds a closure property: "A knowledge space is a knowledge structure that is closed under set union." The educational intuition: if for each topic an expert exists in a class, then with enough effort any student could master all those topics simultaneously. Formally, for any two states in K, their union must also be in K. ## Quasi-Ordinal Knowledge Space / Closure Under Intersection "A quasi-ordinal knowledge space is a knowledge space that is also closed under set intersection." This means the intersection of any two knowledge states is also a feasible state. Such a space forms a distributive lattice under union and intersection. ## Surmise Relation / Prerequisite Partial Order The article describes the concept via the prerequisite partial order. A quasi-ordinal space "can be associated with a distributive lattice," and via Birkhoff's representation theorem, distributive lattices "uniquely correspond to partial orders." Set inclusion defines this partial order on K, "interpretable as an educational prerequirement: if a(⪯)b … then a must be learned before b." ## Well-Graded Knowledge Space / Learning Space "A well-graded knowledge space or learning space is a knowledge space satisfying" the axiom: "If S∈K, then there exists x∈S such that S\{x}∈K." Educationally, "any feasible body of knowledge can be learned one concept at a time." Such a space is an antimatroid. ## Inner and Outer Fringe The outer fringe of a state represents "what the student was ready to learn," while the inner fringe represents "what the student just learned." These are determined by the covering relation of the prerequisite partial order.
