SNAPSHOT: arXiv abs/2111.14380 — The language of pre-topology in knowledge spaces URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.14380 Fetched: 2026-08-13 via WebFetch Authors: Fucai Lin, Xiyan Cao, Jinjin Li --- [Abstract, verbatim segments] "We systematically study some basic properties of the theory of pre-topological spaces, such as, pre-base, subspace, axioms of separation, connectedness, etc. Pre-topology is also known as knowledge space in the theory of knowledge structures." "We discuss the language of axioms of separation of pre-topology in the theory of knowledge spaces, the relation of Alexandroff spaces and quasi ordinal spaces, and the applications of the density of pre-topological spaces in primary items for knowledge spaces." "In particular, we give a characterization of a skill multimap such that the delineate knowledge structure is a knowledge space" — "which gives an answer to a problem in [citations] whenever each item with finitely many competencies" — "moreover, we give an algorithm to find the set of atom primary items for any finite knowledge spaces."
