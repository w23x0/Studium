## 提取正文（关键句逐字）
- "The native NebulaGraph Query Language, also known as nGQL, is a declarative, openCypher-compatible textual query language."
- "NebulaGraph is an open-source, distributed, easily scalable, and native graph database."
- "NebulaGraph is designed in a shared-nothing architecture and supports scaling in and out without interrupting the database service."
- "NebulaGraph is a product based on SSD. Compared with products based on HDD and large memory, it is more suitable for future hardware trends."
- "It is capable of hosting graphs with hundreds of billions of vertices and trillions of edges, and serving queries with millisecond-latency."
