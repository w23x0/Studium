## Abstract（原文摘录）
"The Mizar Mathematical Library (MML) is a rich database of formalized mathematical proofs (see this http URL (http://mizar.org)). Owing to its large size (it contains more than 1100 'articles' summing to nearly 2.5 million lines of text, expressing more than 50000 theorems and 10000 definitions using more than 7000 symbols), the nature of its contents (the MML is slanted toward pure mathematics), and its classical foundations (first-order logic, set theory, natural deduction), the MML is an especially attractive target for research on foundations of mathematics."

"The heart of mizar-items is a method for decomposing the contents of the MML into fine-grained 'items' (e.g., theorem, definition, notation, etc.) and computing dependency relations among these items."

"mizar-items also comes equipped with a website for exploring these dependencies and interacting with them."

## 要点
- MML 规模（2011 口径）：>1100 篇文章、~250 万行、>50000 定理、>10000 定义、>7000 符号。
- 把 MML 分解为细粒度 item（定理/定义/记号等），计算 item 间依赖关系。
- 配套网站可交互探索依赖。
