# Toward Robust GraphRAG: Mitigating Retrieval Drift and Hallucination from Imperfect Knowledge Graphs 来源：https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14828 标题：Toward Robust GraphRAG: Mitigating Retrieval Drift and Hallucination from Imperfect Knowledge Graphs（CS-RAG） 日期：2026-03-16 提交；v2 抓取日期：2026-08-13（WebFetch，A 级原文） ## 核心论断（原文摘录） - 「most existing GraphRAG methods implicitly assume that LLM-constructed KGs provide structural support for evidence chaining. This assumption does not always hold in practice through an empirical analysis.」 - 两类 KG 缺陷：「Spurious noise induces retrieval drift toward plausible but unsupported triples, whereas incomplete information leads to retrieval hallucination by forcing continuation through under-supported graph structure.」 - 缓解 CS-RAG：(1) 把查询规划成「an ordered sequence of executable atomic constraints」做「fine-grained anchor- and relation-aware retrieval」；(2) 「a sufficiency check to decide whether the retrieved evidence can safely induce variable bindings」；(3) 结构支持不足时启用「textual recovery」 - 缓解发生在「during retrieval rather than relying on KG repair」；在三个多跳 QA 基准上「CS-RAG is less sensitive to builder choice and remains stable under controlled KG issue injection」 ## 对 V6 的意义（Q2 红线） - LLM 构建的图含虚假噪声与不完整信息，图结构不能可靠支撑证据链——与 Studium「锚点判等」红线的动机同源 - CS-RAG 的「sufficiency check 决定检索证据能否安全诱导变量绑定」= 不让图结构单独结案，结构不足时退回原文——这是「图/结构只定向不结案、原文兜底」的外部对应物 - 注意：CS-RAG 用「anchor- and relation-aware retrieval」的 anchor 指图谱锚点，非 Studium 的原文锚点——引用时标注，避免混用 ## 等级 A（WebFetch 抓取 arxiv 摘要页）
