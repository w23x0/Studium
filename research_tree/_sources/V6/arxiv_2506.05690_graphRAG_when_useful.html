# arxiv 2506.05690 — When to use Graphs in RAG 来源：https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.05690 （摘要页） + https://arxiv.org/html/2506.05690v3 （HTML 全文） 标题：When to use Graphs in RAG: A Comprehensive Analysis for Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation 作者：Zhishang Xiang, Chuanjie Wu, Qinggang Zhang, Shengyuan Chen, Zijin Hong, Xiao Huang, Jinsong Su 日期：2025-06-06 提交 v1；2026-02-22 修订 v3 GitHub：https://github.com/GraphRAG-Bench/GraphRAG-Benchmark 抓取日期：2026-08-13（WebFetch，A 级原文） ## 摘要（原文摘录） "GraphRAG frequently underperforms vanilla RAG on many real-world tasks" — 引出"图何时有帮助"的问题。 引入 GraphRAG-Bench 基准，覆盖层次化知识检索与深层上下文推理，任务难度从事实检索到创意生成递增，覆盖从构图到检索到生成的完整流水线。系统考察 GraphRAG 超越传统 RAG 的条件与成功原因。 ## 关键发现（HTML 全文摘录） ### 图在什么任务上有害 - "Basic RAG is comparable to or outperforms GraphRAG in simple fact retrieval tasks that does not require complex reasoning across connected concepts." - "extra graph-based processing may introduce redundant or noisy information for simpler queries, degrading answer quality." - 数据：简单事实检索（novel 数据集）RAG+rerank 60.92% 准确率 vs MS-GraphRAG 49.29%。Basic RAG 在简单检索上胜过大多数 GraphRAG 变体。 - "GraphRAG achieves 13.4% lower accuracy on Natural Question compared to vanilla RAG"；"2.3 × higher latency on average." ### 图在什么任务上有用 - "GraphRAG models show a clear advantage in complex reasoning, Contextual Summarize, and creative generation" — 因为"these tasks require bridging the complex relations among multiple concepts, which is naturally a graph structure." - "GraphRAG's advantages emerge clearly as questions grow more complex." - 数据：复杂推理（novel）MS-GraphRAG 50.93% vs RAG+rerank 42.93%；HippoRAG2 53.38%。Contextual Summarize：MS-GraphRAG 64.40% vs RAG+rerank 51.30%。 - 检索侧：HippoRAG Evidence Recall 87.9–90.9%；HippoRAG2 Context Relevance 85.8–87.8%。RAG 在简单问题事实检索上 83.2% Evidence Recall。 ### 成本/令牌开销 - "Compared to vanilla RAG, GraphRAG significantly increases prompt length due to the additional steps involved in knowledge retrieval." - "MS-GraphRAG(global)... reaches a prompt size of up to 4×10⁴ tokens. LightRAG also produces lengthy prompts (≈10⁴ tokens). In contrast, HippoRAG2 maintains a more compact prompt size (≈10³ tokens)." - "MS-GraphRAG(global)'s prompt size expands from 7,800 to 40,000 tokens across tasks of increasing difficulty." - "This excessive token accumulation often introduces redundant information, which in turn degrades context relevance during retrieval." ### 图密度与检索效果 - HippoRAG2 在 novel 数据上平均 2,310 边 / 523 节点（每 10k 语料令牌），medical 上 3,979 边 / 598 节点——更密的图与更好的检索性能相关。 ### 现有基准的缺陷 - HotpotQA 平均实体 39.3、MultiHopRAG 10.1——"far below the threshold of multi-hop reasoning"——不足以测试 GraphRAG 的优势。 ## 实践指南（论文结论） - 分层使用：Level 1 事实检索用 Basic RAG（足够且更高效）；Level 2–4（复杂推理/总结/创意生成）GraphRAG 有可测收益（"connect information across distant text segments, crucial for multi-hop reasoning"）。 - 权衡提示："while GraphRAG improves retrieval breadth, it may also introduce noisy context due to prompt inflation, especially in complex tasks." ## 等级 A（WebFetch 抓取 arxiv 摘要页 + HTML 全文；关键数字直接摘录原文）
