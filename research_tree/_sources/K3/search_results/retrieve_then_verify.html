It often involves identifying relevant companies and fiscal years, locating standardized filing sections, collecting textual and tabular evidence, and checking answers against the original documents. Existing RAG systems, however, usually flatten long filings into unordered chunks, pay limited attention to the typed structure of financial reports, and use fixed text-table fusion strategies without considering query intent.
