Neither audits the reliability of that check itself. We show it is not reliable, and that this matters. However, the faithfulness of these citations -- whether the model genuinely uses a source to generate an answer -- remains a critical, unverified assumption. This paper offers the first mechanistic account of how a large language model decides whether to attach an inline citation while answering a factoid question.
